PHOTOSYNTHESIS
Sample demonstration note for Study Buddy (fictional, not a real student's notes).

What it is:
Photosynthesis is how plants, algae and some bacteria make their own food using light energy.

Word equation:
carbon dioxide + water --(light, chlorophyll)--> glucose + oxygen

Where it happens:
In the chloroplasts, mainly in leaf cells. Chlorophyll is the green pigment that absorbs light.

Two stages:
1. Light-dependent reactions (in the thylakoid membranes): light energy splits water,
   releasing oxygen and making ATP and NADPH.
2. Calvin cycle / light-independent reactions (in the stroma): ATP and NADPH are used
   to turn carbon dioxide into glucose.

Factors that affect the rate:
- Light intensity
- Carbon dioxide concentration
- Temperature

Why it matters:
It produces the oxygen we breathe and is the starting point of most food chains.
